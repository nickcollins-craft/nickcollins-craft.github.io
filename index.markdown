I am currently employed as a lecturer in the [School of Mathematics, Statistics, Chemistry and Physics](https://www.murdoch.edu.au/schools/mathematics-statistics-chemistry-and-physics) at Murdoch University. Here you can find my publications, codes and data sets, as well as links to my various other online presences.
